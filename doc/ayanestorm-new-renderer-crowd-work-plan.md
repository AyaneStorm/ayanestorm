Status:
- 1: written, asrenderer_test passes. Cluster blocks in their own table (`ClusterBlocks`), vertex blocks sized by vertices only, groups one workgroup per binding. Log: 'skin work run/in use' for palette, vertex and cluster blocks. Not done: pose age per avatar.
- 2: written, not built. Tagged hook `ASRWorld::avatarAnimated()` in `LLVOAvatar::updateCharacter()` after its early out. `poseSkeleton()` reads joints only when animated, period 1, render origin moved, new, or every 64 frames (sweep); body weights only then. Reused scratch vectors. Not done: the per-frame alive set and spatial-extents update for every avatar (doc 8).

- 3: debug view written, asrenderer_test passes: `ASRendererDebugView` 1 normals, 2 albedo, 3 alpha mode (grey opaque, green cut-out, red blended), carried on `SceneCamera3D::debugView`. Found: the resolve uses no normal maps, and Mac OIT faces get no light (blended faces unlit next to lit opaque ones). User's views: albedo clean, faces cut-out, normals banded per mesh piece; RenderVolumeLODFactor 8 changes nothing (not LOD). Cause: `skinNormal()` turned normals with the palette matrix, which holds the bind shape (can scale unevenly); the viewer turns them by the bind shape's inverse transpose on the CPU. Fixed: cofactor per joint, sign kept outward, normalized per joint. Also fixed: the resolve multiplied the instance cofactor on the wrong side (`mat3()` takes columns), turning rotated objects' normals backwards. asrenderer_test passes; the user confirmed in the viewer that the patches are gone.

- Flickering layers (lips, eyes): debug views 4 (cluster colours) and 5 (occlusion off) showed a depth fight, not culling. Cause: rigged sources were snorm16 over the whole object's box, much coarser than the asset's per-face grid, so layers a few microns apart crossed. Rigged source positions are now float4 (`FloatPosition`, tagged address, `vertexCountOf()`). Test passes. User: much better on own avatar, still on another. Second cause: the ID tie rank moves a face up to 2.4e-4 of its distance (0.5 mm at 2 m), more than a layer's offset. Rigged faces now rank by face index only (0-7, `writeFace()`); static faces keep the ID rank. User: lipstick (blended) now shows but looks fullbright, some specks left. Then: rigged rank = pass (blended 2, cut-out 1, opaque 0) then face, as OpenGL's pass order settles equal depth (LEQUAL); Mac OIT now takes the resolve's placeholder light with skinned normals (`placeholderLight()`, `skinNormal()`, `worldNormalOf()` moved to common). Tests pass. User mode 3: holes in the blended lipstick where cut-out lips win, despite the pass rank. Cause: no `invariant gl_Position`, so the visibility and Mac OIT vertex shaders could round the same position differently (microns at world scale). Now one `scenePosition()` in common, `invariant gl_Position` in both vertex shaders, Metal compiled with `-fpreserve-invariance` (Mac unverified). Then, from doc/renderer-avatar-lip-flicker-diagnosis.md point 1: render space is region coordinates, where a float steps in 7-15 microns, and each vertex went through a world position. Now `makePalette()` moves the joint to the skeleton centre before the bind matrices multiply it, and `scenePosition()` places vertices camera-relative (instance offset minus camera, then the camera's clip position added). Tests pass. Not done: OIT keys ignore the rank (blended over blended only; its 22-bit bins are coarser than a rank step anyway); ties between separate attachments of the same pass and face number.

- 4: not needed for now. Every LOD up to the wanted one is asked at once, built coarse to fine, and a rigged face shows the coarsest built. In a crowd the log showed 36 objects with nothing yet and 23 LODs waiting, cleared within seconds; nobody stuck in a system body. Reopen with the log's counters if it comes back. New gap: flexible prims are left out (73).

- 6: textures were not short of memory (4238 of 6025 MB) but shrank 26898 times in a session: each feedback batch samples other pixels and replaced the needed level, and a texture missed for 0.5 s dropped two levels. Now the largest level holds 4 s (`gpuLevelAt`, takeFeedback), and seen-lately is 4 s. Log line 'geometry MB' splits chunks/free, tables (deformed positions, clusters) and lists, to choose what to cut from the 3.2 GB. Not built in the viewer yet.

- 6, user build: texture cycling gone. Log: chunks 2176 MB, tables 1101 (deformed positions 512, clusters 512, both doubled), lists 63. Written, asrenderer_test passes:
  - tables and ranges grow by a quarter, not double (`ensureTable()`, `allocateRange()`, cluster ranges);
  - skinned copies only near the view: `SceneQueue::setSkeletonResident()`, store `setResident()` / `allocateDeformed()` / `releaseDeformed()`; a parked binding is written empty. Viewer parks an avatar outside the view's corner angle plus 45 degrees for 3 s (`setAnimationPeriods()`). Shadows will need them back (P4);
  - rigged LOD size by own size: reverted. Outfits lost parts at 4 and 8 m: rigged meshes' coarser LODs are often empty, as the viewer measures them by the whole avatar. Rigged LOD stays avatar-sized for good.
  - User: slowdown to 13-20 ms GPU; skin clusters/groups and group culling x15, pyramid unchanged; Task Manager: VRAM full, 1 GB shared. Paging. Textures 5.0 GB + geometry 3.2 GB. Fix 1: the texture limit is at most what textures hold plus what the driver has left under 90% of its budget (`getTextureMemory()` in asrviewer.cpp), so the viewer's discard bias shrinks textures before paging. Log: driver usage/budget on the texture memory line. User: no more shared memory, 240 fps outdoors, but own and near avatars' textures stuck blurry until refreshed: images were refused (`textureFits()`) and updates frozen (`sFreezeImageUpdates`) at the steering limit. Now both use a hard limit, the driver's full budget; the 90% line only steers the discard bias. Then: under pressure the bias weighs by the GPU-needed level (`judgeTexture()`): 512+ texels keep detail, 64 or fewer take the full bias (up to 1.5 times its exponent), near ones give only when the bias passes 3. Not built.
  - Geometry, written, not built: `memoryPressure()` (driver usage over 90% of its budget); `heldLod()`: the viewer's LOD, one coarser for static objects beyond 8 m under pressure, never for rigged; `wantMeshLods()` asks up to it; `evictMeshLods()` once a second drops a mesh's finest built LOD when no user holds it for 30 s (3 s under pressure), re-applies users, removes the geometry once nothing is left to apply. Log: 'unwanted LODs freed so far', 'memory short'. Not done: chunks and tables never shrink, so freed space is reused but not given back to the driver (release empty chunks; compact tables).
  - User: 10 fps, driver usage over budget, chunks grew to 2752 MB. `evictMeshLods()` removed: dropped LODs were rebuilt into new chunks while freed space stayed as holes. Kept: under pressure, static objects beyond 8 m ask for one LOD less. Vulkan allocator: 64 MB blocks (was 256), images of 8 MB or more get their own memory, so shrunk textures give memory back. Near textures give way from bias 2.5 (was 3). Log: allocator held/used. Tests pass.
  - User: memory now fine (7.4 of 11.3 GB, allocator 7.3 held / 6.4 used), but avatars 15-20 m behind did not show, then broken system bodies; 48 parked. Parking off by default (`ASRendererParkAvatars`) until un-parking real avatars is understood. Also fixed: `heldLod()` took objects not yet set up as scenery; it now spares anything an avatar or animesh wears, from the volume itself.
  - Cause found: avatars right in view counted as out of it. Ranking and parking read the drawable's spatial extents and `mDistanceWRTCamera`, and avatar LOD distance read `getPositionGroup()`; only the viewer's own pipeline keeps those current. All three now use `getRenderPosition()` (and the anim extents for the radius, at least 1.5 m). Parking stays off by default until confirmed.
  - Avatars not drawn at all began with the build that changed the Vulkan allocator (64 MB blocks, dedicated images of 8 MB+); parking off and live positions did not fix it. Allocator change reverted.
  - Real cause of avatars in view not rezzing (out of view ones did): discard bias pinned at 4 by the 90% steering line, which geometry alone (3.9 GB) kept crossed; in-view textures follow GPU feedback divided by the bias, and undrawn ones count as hidden (/16), so they starved. Steering now uses the driver's full budget (the viewer's 85% target is the margin).
  - Still in-view avatars not rezzing. `judgeTexture()` judged a texture in view but never GPU-sampled as hidden (/16); a mesh body cut out by a missing bake is never sampled, so its bake was never wanted. Never-seen textures now keep the faces' size.
  - Club at 60 fps: GPU 12-17 ms, driver 10376 of 10591 MB, allocator 10.2 GB held / 8.8 used, groups early 3.5 ms (paging). The allocator and parking reverts were wrong guesses: both restored (64 MB blocks, own memory for images of 8 MB+, parking on by default).
  - Was next: LOD admission: keep only LODs some object wants, free finer ones after ~30 s, cap finer LODs for far objects when geometry has no room (rigged ones by their avatar, never by their own size).
